About SpendWise

Built by people who understand money and value privacy.

We believe financial clarity shouldn't come at the cost of your privacy.

SpendWise was born from a simple frustration: most expense tracking apps want access to your bank accounts, read your SMS, or sell your data. We built something different—a tool that respects your privacy while giving you the insights you need to make better financial decisions.

Our team is a small group of developers, designers, and financial enthusiasts who care deeply about creating tools that put you in control. We're not backed by venture capital, and we don't monetize your data.
