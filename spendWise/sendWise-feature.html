SpendWise feature overview

Features that keep your money crystal clear every month.

Built for everyday people who want clarity, not complexity.

SpendWise keeps things intentionally simple: you enter what you spend, and it returns clear, focused views of where your money goes, how your habits shift over time, and how much you can safely save without giving up control of your data.

Daily Expense Tracking

Log your expenses in seconds, from any device.

  • Add amount, category, and notes.
  • Track spending day by day.
  • Desktop and mobile optimized.

Weekly & Monthly Reports

See your money clearly with visual summaries.

  • Automatic spending summaries.
  • Category‑wise breakdowns.
  • Spot patterns at a glance.

Savings Rule Calculator

Plan smarter with rule‑based guidance.

  • Apply 50‑30‑20 rules easily.
  • Create custom saving goals.
  • See your guilt‑free allowance.

Privacy‑First by Design

Your data stays yours. No bank connections.

  • No bank or card access required.
  • No SMS reading or scanning.
  • Manual entry for total control.
